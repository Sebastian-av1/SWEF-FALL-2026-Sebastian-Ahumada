# STAR response

**Question:** Tell me about a time you faced a challenge while working on a project or with a team. What did you do, and what was the result?

## Situation
In one of my accounting classes, we had a group project where we had to analyze a company's financial statements and recommend whether or not to buy ownership in it. It was supposed to be split between everyone in the group, but on the day it was due, my groupmates hadn't done their parts.


## Task
The project was still due that day, and I didn't want all of us to fail it. So I decided the only realistic option was to do the whole thing myself.


## Action
I went through the company's financial statements on my own, did the analysis, and wrote up our recommendation. It was a lot of work for one day. Then, right when I finished, I accidentally deleted the entire thing.

I had two choices: look for an excuse and ask for an extension, or just start over. I started over. Since I'd already worked through all of it once, the second time went a lot faster, and I rebuilt the whole project from scratch.


## Result
I turned the project in on time. And honestly, doing the analysis twice made me way more comfortable with accounting — reading financial statements started to actually make sense to me. The bigger lesson for me was about perseverance: when something goes wrong, the fastest way out is usually just to put your head down and fix it, instead of looking for a way around it.
